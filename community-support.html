Our approach

Great meetups don't happen by accident. Ruby Europe believes the strongest communities are built locally.

Every meetup creates opportunities for developers to learn, exchange ideas, make new connections, and inspire others to start something themselves.

That's why we're here to support the people who make those meetups happen!

Whether you need help getting started, finding a venue, connecting with speakers, promoting your event, or covering some of the costs - we're here to help.

The program

What support can you get?

The Ruby Europe Community Support is a lightweight program created for Ruby meetup organizers across Europe.

Depending on your needs, we can help with:

  • Up to €300 in funding
  • Promotional support
  • Organizational support with planning, venues, and speakers
  • Guidance from experienced community organizers
