A network of peers

Who we are

Ruby Europe is a non-profit initiative connecting the people who sustain Ruby communities across Europe.

We bring meetup organizers, speakers, and developers into a practical peer network where experience, introductions, and ideas can move between local groups.

We help when support is useful and stay out of the way when it is not. Every community keeps its own identity, priorities, and pace.
