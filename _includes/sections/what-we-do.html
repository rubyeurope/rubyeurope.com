What we do

Two pillars carry our work.

Community

Community is at the heart of Ruby Europe.

We connect meetups, organizers, and speakers across Europe, helping ideas, experience, and people move freely between local groups.

By strengthening these connections, we support existing communities and help them grow together.

Education

Learning. Mentoring. Growing Ruby.

Through initiatives like Ruby Europe Academy, online courses, and mentoring, we help new developers join the community and make Ruby more accessible.

By nurturing skills and knowledge, we strengthen communities and contribute to the long-term growth of Ruby in Europe.
