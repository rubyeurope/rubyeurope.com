Shared capacity

What we do

We make a European peer network useful through two focused areas of work.

01

Community

Connect the people doing the work.

We help organizers, speakers, and ideas travel between cities. Local groups gain peers to learn from, collaborate with, and call on when useful.

02

Education

Make Ruby knowledge easier to share.

Through courses, mentoring, and community-led initiatives, we help people learn Ruby and give experienced practitioners more ways to pass knowledge on.
