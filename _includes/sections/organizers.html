For organizers

Joining Ruby Europe means becoming part of a large European network while keeping your identity. Your event keeps its name, brand, and story.

Visibility & reach

  • Promotion through Ruby Europe channels.
  • Easier discovery for new attendees, speakers, and partners.
  • Feature your content on Ruby Europe's YouTube and social platforms.

Organizational support

  • Access to shared know-how from experienced organizers.
  • Practical guidance for running meetups and events.
  • Hands-on mentoring when starting or scaling an initiative.

Speakers & content

  • Access to a growing pool of Ruby speakers across Europe.
  • Easier speaker exchange between cities and countries.
  • Amplification of recorded talks beyond your local audience.

Trust & credibility

  • Use of the "Part of Ruby Europe" badge.
  • A clear signal your event is part of a recognized European ecosystem.
  • Extra credibility for new meetups and organizers.

How joining works

  1. Reach out. Let's talk about your meetup or initiative.
  2. Connect. We connect you with the network and explore how to support.
  3. Grow. Your meetup becomes part of the European network.
