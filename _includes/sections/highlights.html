  2. Multi-city initiative

    The Spain Triangle Project

    The project connected Ruby communities in Barcelona, Valencia, and Madrid through linked local meetups. Barcelona and Madrid hosted November stops; Valencia's event was postponed after severe flooding, keeping the project grounded in local circumstances.
