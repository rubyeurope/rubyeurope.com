It doesn't have to be about money

Ruby Europe exists to support the European Ruby community. When a local meetup thrives — people meet, learn and build together — the whole ecosystem grows with it. So we help on whatever front you're short on. Sometimes that's a company willing to lend a room. Sometimes it's finding someone to give a talk. Sometimes it's covering the real costs. Often it's a mix.

If what you're missing is a venue or a speaker and we can help you find one, that might be all you need. That's a good outcome, not a smaller one.

What we can help with

  • A venue or host

    We'll put you in touch with a company or space willing to host your meetup.

  • A speaker

    Someone gave a talk in another city this month. We can ask them to give it in yours, or pass your call for papers along the network.

  • Funding

    When money is the blocker: the room, the food, a speaker's travel, equipment, promotion.

  • Getting started

    Running a first meetup, or waking up a group that went quiet. We've done both.

Common questions

Do I need a registered organisation?
No. Individuals and informal groups are welcome. Invoicing is only sorted out after we've agreed to support you.
What if I only need a venue or a speaker?
Perfect. Tell us what's missing and we'll try to connect you with it. If it turns out you don't need money at all, even better.
Does my event need to be big?
Not at all. A small local meetup is exactly the kind of thing we want to help happen.
Do I have to credit anyone?
No. If you'd like to share how it went afterwards we'd love to hear it, but it's entirely up to you.
