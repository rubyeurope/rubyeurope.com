Three things, done properly

  • We connect the people behind the groups

    We help talks, calls for papers and introductions travel between cities, so a talk given in Vienna can be given again in Kraków and nobody starts from nothing.

  • We cover what a meetup is missing

    A room, pizza, a projector, a speaker's train ticket. Whatever is standing between an organiser and the next evening.

  • We take Ruby on tour

    Spain Triangle

    Bringing Ruby meetups in Spain back to life. Barcelona, Valencia and Madrid, linked by train, with speakers travelling from one to the next.

    Ruby + AI

    Making the case for Ruby as a good language for building AI products. It started in Warsaw with PLRUG in October 2024, then went to London, Berlin and Paris.
